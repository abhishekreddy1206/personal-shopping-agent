# Shopping Trigger Rules

Babji should route a message into the shopping agent when the request clearly asks for one or more of the following:

- find a product deal
- compare prices
- track a product price
- show shopping history
- show shopping watchlist
- check whether a product dropped in price
- hunt for coupons or better offers
- search in marketplace mode
- decide whether to buy now or wait

## Example trigger phrases
- "find me the best deal on ..."
- "track this if it drops below ..."
- "show my watchlist"
- "show my recent shopping searches"
- "best running shoes under 120"
- "search in marketplace mode for ..."
- "hunt aggressively for deals on ..."

## Non-trigger examples
Do not route messages like:
- general life advice
- job search questions
- coding help
- meal planning
- unrelated references to money or cost without shopping intent
